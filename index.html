About

Senior ERP architect and Oracle HCM leader with 18 years of independent transformation delivery across healthcare, aviation, and FMCG — turning Oracle complexity into business outcomes through integrations, analytics, and AI that frees up HR rather than adds to its workload.

Based in Dubai, I'm a Business Applications Principal with 18 years in IT and 15+ in Oracle Applications — working across the full Oracle Fusion HCM and Oracle E-Business Suite landscape as a techno-functional practitioner. I don't just configure — I build VBCS pages, write Fast Formula, design HCM Extracts, wire OIC integrations, and set up the data pipelines that sit behind the dashboards leaders actually use.

My integration work connects Oracle Fusion to WhatsApp for real-time employee notifications, SharePoint for document and approval flows, biometric attendance systems, and SAP Financials — surfacing it all through Power BI and OAC dashboards covering leave forecasting, attrition risk, headcount projections, and recruitment pipeline. Across the Fusion suite I've delivered hands-on work in Core HR · Payroll · Absence · ORC · LMS · Goals & Performance · Talent Management · Succession Planning · Benefits · Compensation, alongside Redwood UX rollouts and AI Agents live in production.

My belief in AI is straightforward: it should enable people, not restrict them. The four AI Agents I built — Leave Assistant, Policy RAG Agent, Workflow Agent, Symptoms App — are designed to reduce friction, surface answers faster, and free up HR teams to focus on work that matters. I see AI as a lever for revenue generation and employee wellness, not another layer of controls on how people work.

I'm actively looking to connect with teams investing in new technology sectors — healthcare AI, enterprise automation, HR tech — and I'm part of advisory communities that support AI tools literacy, helping practitioners across organisations learn how to build and use AI with confidence. Currently pursuing a DBA specialising in Generative AI at Golden Gate University (AACSB accredited, 2026–2029), with research focused on enterprise AI deployment gaps.
